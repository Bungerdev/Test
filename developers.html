Put your game on a disc.

No upfront cost and no exclusivity. Keep selling digitally wherever you like. A warp.cd edition just gives players something they can hold.

How it works

How an order works.

We only make a disc when someone buys one, so there is nothing to pre-order or warehouse.

01
You apply
Send the game, a build or store link, and a price between $15 and $30. Takes about five minutes.
02
We set it up
We prep the disc artwork and an offline installer, then list your edition on the shelf.
03
Someone orders
We format a 4.7GB DVD, burn the game to it, and print the design on the disc and the case sleeve.
04
It ships
The disc goes out and the buyer covers shipping, usually about $7. You keep 80%, paid monthly.
The split

Price a copy, see your cut.

Developers keep 80% of net receipts on every disc sold. Our 20% covers burning and printing each order. There is no upfront cost.
