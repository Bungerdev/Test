Questions

Good to know.

You receive 80% of net receipts on every copy sold — that's the sale price after payment-processing fees. A $20 disc pays you $16. Our 20% covers disc pressing, printed packaging, storage, and shipping logistics.

We do. Orders are printed on demand and shipped worldwide, and we handle customer support and any returns. You never touch a padded envelope.

A standard PC disc carrying an offline installer for your build. Players can install and play without a launcher or an internet connection. We support Windows, macOS, and Linux builds.

Always. There's no exclusivity. Keep selling on Steam, itch, or anywhere else — a warp.cd edition is simply an extra, physical way for people to own your game. You can pull the edition whenever you like.

None. Because we print on demand, there's nothing to pre-order or warehouse. We only make a disc when someone buys one, so there's no risk of unsold stock.

Payouts go out monthly with a clear statement of copies sold and receipts. Set a threshold that suits you, and we'll send your share by your preferred method.
